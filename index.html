FAQ

Questions, answered.

What do I need to know before starting?

Functions, classes or structs, pointers and references, standard containers, and basic Git. A short diagnostic before the course spots any gaps.

How are lessons delivered?

Online, one-on-one, scheduled around your time zone (Jacob is in US Pacific time).

What setup do I need?

Linux or WSL, GCC 11+ or Clang 14+, CMake 3.24+, Ninja and Git. macOS works for most concepts.

Is the capstone defense included?

Yes, the course is 14 sessions plus a capstone defense.

Do I need any AI or machine-learning background?

No. The course is about native C++ engineering; the LLM agent is just the realistic, hostile client your code has to stay safe against.
